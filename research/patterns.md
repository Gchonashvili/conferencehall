# 6 Patterns Shared Across All 8 Sites

Source: `research/research.md`. The eight sites are Tagvenue, Peerspace, Giggster, HeadBox, Hire Space, VenueScanner, ExCeL London and Marina Bay Sands (MBS).

## 1. Everything is organised by event type
Visitors say what they're planning before they say anything else.

- **Tagvenue, VenueScanner:** event-type tiles on the homepage, and an event-types directory (VenueScanner tiles also show venue counts).
- **Peerspace, Giggster:** browse by activity or type (`/plan/activities`, `/book/types`).
- **HeadBox:** coverage runs from a 10-person meeting to a 10,000-person conference, and the brief starts from event type.
- **Hire Space:** filters by venue type.
- **ExCeL:** filters by event type (award ceremony, corporate, exhibition and so on).
- **MBS:** the "Explore our venue" grid is by event type (meetings, exhibitions, dinners, weddings, incentives).

**For Conferencehall:** make event type the first navigation axis, not a buried filter.

## 2. Capacity and headcount are first-class data
Guest count is either a search input or the headline fact on each space.

- **Tagvenue, VenueScanner:** guest count in the search bar.
- **Hire Space:** "people" in the search bar.
- **Peerspace:** capacity visible in results.
- **HeadBox:** the brief starts from event size.
- **ExCeL:** delegate-number filter (0–20 up to 1,000+) and capacity charts per layout.
- **MBS:** capacity icons per space.
- **Giggster:** the weakest fit. Its documented filters are location, date, price and features, and capacity isn't called out.

**For Conferencehall:** capacity in the hero search and on every hall card. Add layout-specific numbers (theatre, classroom, banquet) on hall pages.

## 3. Credibility is proven on the front page, not just claimed
Every site shows evidence early.

- **Reviews and ratings:** Tagvenue 4.73, Giggster 4.81, VenueScanner's Reviews.io badge under the search bar, Peerspace two-sided reviews.
- **Client logos:** HeadBox (Accenture, KPMG), Hire Space (Facebook, Uber), VenueScanner (Google, Amazon).
- **Scale stats:** listing counts and user counts on the marketplaces.
- **Heritage and track record:** ExCeL's 2012 Olympics and 25 years, and MBS's success stories and scale (250 meeting rooms, 45,000 delegates).

**For Conferencehall:** at launch, use what's real: verified-listing badges, hall counts, and named clients or events hosted. Don't fake review scores.

## 4. Visuals do the selling: photography, galleries, virtual tours
Visitors can see the space before enquiring.

- **Peerspace, Giggster:** full-bleed photography and user galleries ("Made in Peerspace", "Created with Giggster").
- **Tagvenue:** image galleries.
- **HeadBox:** a 3D Studio with tours.
- **Hire Space:** 3D tours.
- **ExCeL:** virtual tours and floor plans.
- **MBS:** self-serve 360° tours and galleries.
- **VenueScanner:** the weakest fit. Its listing pages aren't documented for tours, though the hero is photographic.

**For Conferencehall:** require good photos as a listing minimum. Treat 360° tours as an upgrade for halls that can supply them.

## 5. A human enquiry path sits beside the self-serve one
No site relies only on a button. Each pairs self-serve with a person or a message channel.

- **Tagvenue:** direct messaging and one enquiry to multiple venues.
- **Peerspace:** host messaging and custom quotes, plus concierge.
- **Giggster:** concierge / "connect with an expert".
- **HeadBox:** brief-led matching by AI and human experts.
- **Hire Space:** a free named-agent concierge.
- **VenueScanner:** in-platform inbox, plus a free concierge (VenueScanner for Business).
- **ExCeL:** live chat plus a long list of named sales contacts.
- **MBS:** regional sales offices and dedicated inboxes.

**For Conferencehall:** ship self-serve enquiry first, with a "tell us your brief and we'll help" form and a phone or WhatsApp option. Phone and messaging suit the Georgian market.

## 6. Location is a primary anchor
Each site treats where as one of the first things to choose or explain.

- **Tagvenue, Peerspace, Giggster, VenueScanner:** city tiles or location directories, often with per-city landing pages and listing counts.
- **Hire Space:** map-based search over curated UK venues.
- **ExCeL:** a "Why London" section and connectivity messaging.
- **MBS:** "central location" and "20 minutes from Changi Airport" in its feature panels.
- **HeadBox:** the weakest fit. It is location-agnostic in messaging, but location is part of every brief.

**For Conferencehall:** city tiles for Tbilisi, Batumi and Kutaisi, city landing pages for SEO, and a map view. Add getting-here and nearby-hotels info on hall pages.

## Near-misses: common, but not on all 8
These are worth knowing about, but they don't qualify.

| Feature | Where it appears | Where it doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront pricing | Tagvenue, VenueScanner, Peerspace, Giggster | HeadBox and Hire Space (quote-based), ExCeL and MBS (enquiry-only) |
| "List your venue" host path | All 6 marketplaces | Neither convention center |
| Downloadable spec sheets | ExCeL, MBS | All 6 marketplaces |
| Shortlist / compare | Tagvenue, VenueScanner, HeadBox | The rest |

Upfront pricing is the strongest differentiator among the marketplaces. It isn't a universal pattern, so treat it as a competitive choice rather than table stakes.
